# TindaTrack usability test plan

## Participants

Test with at least five sari-sari store owners, including two older adults and two people who do not regularly use business applications.

## Rules

- Give the participant an Android phone with sample products.
- Explain only that TindaTrack records products, stock, and sales.
- Do not demonstrate the workflow before the test.
- If the participant is stuck for more than 30 seconds, record where and provide the minimum help needed.
- Treat hesitation, wrong taps, and misunderstood labels as design findings.

## Core tasks

1. Add Coke with 20 pieces, a ₱15 cost, and a ₱20 selling price.
2. Record a sale of two Coke.
3. Confirm that 18 Coke remain.
4. Find today's sales and estimated profit.
5. Add one pack of Coke stock.
6. Correct a wrong physical count.
7. Write “Buy Coke tomorrow” in Daily Note.
8. Record a store expense.
9. Record an utang and a partial payment.
10. Create a backup file.

## Record for each task

- Completed without help: Yes/No
- Time to complete
- Wrong taps
- Help requested
- Words or buttons that were misunderstood
- Participant comments

## MVP success criteria

- At least 80% of participants complete Tasks 1–7 without help.
- A one-product sale takes no more than five intentional taps after opening Add Sale.
- No participant manually adjusts stock after recording a sale.
- Every participant can identify today's sales and low-stock warning.
- No critical data loss or negative-stock result occurs.
